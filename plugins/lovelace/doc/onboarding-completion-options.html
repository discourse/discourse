A lighter finish to member onboarding

These earlier alternatives are retained as a design reference. Option 2, “Celebrate, then close,” is now implemented in the member checklist and admin preview. These standalone demos do not change member progress or community settings.

Each demo starts at two of three items complete. Select the final item to try the finish, then use Replay to compare again. Reloading this document resets all demos.
